# 01 — Problem Analysis: Civic Grievance Resolution in Urban India

## 1. Problem Statement Overview (PS02)
Citizen grievances are the primary interface between urban residents and municipal governance. However, municipal grievance portals across India (including Pune Municipal Corporation) face deep structural friction:
1. **Unstructured & Fragmented Ingestion**: Citizens describe issues with varying emotional intensity, colloquial expressions, and code-mixed vernacular languages (Marathi, Hindi, Hinglish), making traditional keyword-based systems fail.
2. **Missing Essential Details**: Over 60% of initial complaints omit critical operational details (e.g., exact landmark, duration of outage, pole numbers), requiring tedious manual follow-ups or causing premature ticket rejection.
3. **Department Siloing & Misrouting**: Municipal governance is partitioned across specialized departments. Citizens cannot accurately diagnose whether waterlogged streets belong to Road, Stormwater, or Public Health, causing misdirected assignments and inter-departmental blame shifting.
4. **Opaque SLA Tracking**: Citizens receive static acknowledgment IDs without transparent visibility into operational stages, leading to repeated filing of the same grievance.
5. **Redundant Duplicate Inundation**: A single infrastructure failure (e.g., a burst water main in Sector 5) generates dozens of isolated complaints, congesting municipal workflows.
